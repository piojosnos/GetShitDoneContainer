- Tracer slice proven end to end in the dev sandbox: `communication.md` (merged from the AGENTS.md communication section and three feedback memories, dashes removed, no frontmatter, generated-file header on line 1) is copied by the hook and shows up in Claude's `/context` as `| User | <config>/rules/communication.md |`.

## Decisions Made

- List file name `.best-practices-skills`, as the plan chose.
- Hook failure bypasses `die()` (its compose hint is wrong for a hook failure) and prints its own `[sbx] ERROR` line.
