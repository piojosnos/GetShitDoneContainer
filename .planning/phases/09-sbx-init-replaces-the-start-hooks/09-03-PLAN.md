<objective>
Give `state/` back to `sandbox` as an init step, sharing one scan with the self-check, and make the self-check strict about root-owned state. Covers INIT-06 and the D-13 tolerance removal, per D-06 and D-13. Folds the pending todo "The GSD sync leaves files owned by root in state/claude".

Purpose: the GSD installer leaves about 300 root-owned files in `state/claude` on Docker Desktop; the step makes the sandbox's own files the sandbox's again after every sync.
Output: `base/selfcheck/root_owned_scan.py` (moved from the check), `base/init/steps/800_state_owner.py`, the strict check, and their tests.

Discretion choices recorded here: the walk skips the same cache folders the self-check skips (`cache/npm`, `cache/uv`, `m2`); the step uses `os.lchown` and never follows a link; at most three scan rounds.

Flagged assumption (RESEARCH A1, Mac only): on Docker Desktop the sandbox user can `lchown` a root-owned file back to itself (the 2026-11-08 todo records it setting a file's owner to root and back). On a plain Linux bind mount a non-root user cannot, and the step FAILs with the Mac command. H-35 (plan 09-11) and the phase gate (plan 09-13) prove it on the Mac; if it is false, the gate's result becomes a gap-closure plan, not an improvised change.
</objective>
