Title: `chore: shell diet`

### What changed

- shellcheck 0.11.0 and hadolint 2.15.1 are pinned by index digest in versions.env, copied into sbx-base, and run by the guard (a missing tool fails the rule).

- The entrypoint self-test keeps only cases backed by a real failure.
