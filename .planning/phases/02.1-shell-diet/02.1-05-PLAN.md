## Conventions carried into every edit

- The guard may run on the Mac shell: stock bash 3.2 and BSD tools (no `sed -i`, `grep -P`, `readlink -f`, `mapfile`, `declare -A`; `sed -E` with `#` delimiters; `\|` in an ERE is a literal pipe in GNU and BSD grep, keep the pipe and substitution regexes byte for byte).
- Braces always, blank lines around control flow, full variable names, ASCII only, no planning IDs in code, comments or docs, no dashes as punctuation, punctuation outside closing quotes.
- Comment shape (D-11, D-12): rule line = `#`, a space and 78 hyphens (80 columns) at column 0; banner = rule, one title line (at most 80 columns), rule; optional why block of at most two lines, closed by a rule; file header right after the shebang in the same shape (purpose title; usage plus at most one other line). Each rule's banner title is `rule_name: what it protects`.
- In the agent tool shell `grep` is a function that runs ugrep; verify commands use `command grep`. Prototype a new grep with `bash -c` or `bash file`, never in the tool shell, and prove it with a planted case.
