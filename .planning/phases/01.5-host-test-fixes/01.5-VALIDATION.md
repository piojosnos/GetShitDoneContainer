## Sampling Rate

- **After every red commit:** the targeted group exits non-zero with the expected FAIL line; `bash tests/guard/run-all.sh` and `bash -n` on touched files.
- **After every green commit:** the targeted group passes, then the full suite command.
- **After every plan wave:** full suite command and `git diff --check`.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** full suite green in the dev sandbox, then `bash tests/host/run-all.sh` on the Mac.
- **Max feedback latency:** 60 seconds (dev sandbox suites).
